The story

A lonely boy named Elliott finds a gentle alien hiding behind his house, left behind when his spaceship had to blast off without him. Elliott and his brother and sister hide E.T. from the grown-ups and help him build a machine to phone home. But the longer E.T. stays on Earth, the weaker he gets, and government agents are closing in.

E.T. phone home! Did you know...

  • Elliott lures E.T. with a trail of Reese's Pieces. After the movie came out, sales of the candy went through the roof.
  • E.T.'s croaky voice was mostly performed by Pat Welsh, a California grandma with a wonderfully raspy voice.
  • E.T.'s face was designed from three real faces: the poet Carl Sandburg, Albert Einstein and Ernest Hemingway.
  • Spielberg shot the movie in order from start to finish, so when the kids say goodbye to E.T., the tears are real.
