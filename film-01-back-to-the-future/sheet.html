The story

Teenager Marty McFly gets zapped back to 1955 in a time machine Doc Brown built out of a DeLorean sports car. By accident, Marty stops his own parents from falling in love. Now he has to get them back together and race to the clock tower for his one shot at getting home.

Great Scott! Did you know...

  • The DeLorean needs to hit exactly 88 mph, powered by 1.21 gigawatts, to break the time barrier.
  • Michael J. Fox filmed his TV show all day and this movie all night. He barely slept for months!
  • Another actor, Eric Stoltz, filmed Marty for weeks before Michael J. Fox took over the part.
  • Doc's dog in 1955 is Copernicus and in 1985 is Einstein: both named after famous scientists.
