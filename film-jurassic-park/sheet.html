The story

A billionaire named John Hammond builds an island park full of REAL dinosaurs, brought back to life using ancient DNA. He invites two dinosaur scientists, a mathematician, and his own grandkids for the very first tour. But when the fences go down, everyone learns the hard way that dinosaurs do not follow park rules.

Life finds a way! Did you know...

  • The T-rex roar is a mix of real animal sounds: a baby elephant, an alligator, and a tiger.
  • When rain soaked the robot T-rex, it started shaking on its own and got even scarier.
  • The velociraptor calls came from geese, an African crane, and tortoises: real animals doing very un-scary things.
  • Spielberg finished this movie from Poland, where he was already filming his next one, Schindler's List.
